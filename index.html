I work at the intersection of analytics, product, and engineering — designing KPI frameworks that survive scale, building behavioural segmentation that drives measurable engagement, and owning the data quality standards that leadership decisions actually depend on.

For the last six years I led analytics at CropBytes, a blockchain gaming platform. I joined as the early analytics hire and was part of the team that scaled the product from a couple of thousand users to over 650,000. Along the way I designed an in-app notification system using behavioural segmentation that delivered a 60% uplift in engagement, built decision-critical dashboards used in quarterly roadmap planning, and acted as the cross-functional accountability point for data quality.

I'm now completing an MSc in Computer Science at Aberystwyth University (graduating September 2026). My dissertation builds a production-style fraud detection pipeline on the IEEE-CIS dataset, comparing whether structured feature engineering through dbt measurably improves XGBoost classifier accuracy versus raw transaction inputs.

I'll be eligible for the UK Graduate Visa from September 2026 — no employer sponsorship required for the first two years post-graduation. I'm targeting Analytics Engineering, Senior Data Analyst, and Product Analyst roles in UK fintech, iGaming, and blockchain analytics.
