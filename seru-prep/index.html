SERU Prep

An Android practice app for London private hire (PHV) driver applicants preparing for TfL's Safety, Equality and Regulatory Understanding (SERU) assessment.

What you get

  • 279 practice questions across all 11 PHV Driver Handbook topics, each with a clear explanation
  • Timed mock tests: 37 questions, 45 minutes, 60% pass mark
  • Multiple choice, sentence completion and reading comprehension questions
  • A readiness score and results by topic
  • My mistakes and saved questions for quick revision
  • Key-fact flashcards
  • Works offline, with no account needed

Free to try

Practise three full topics, sample questions from every other topic, one full mock test and 12 flashcards free. One payment unlocks everything.

Help test the app

SERU Prep is in testing before its public release. You'll need an Android phone running Android 7 or newer.

  1. Join the testers group, using the Google account on your Android phone.
  2. Open the tester link on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install SERU Prep from Google Play and try some questions and a mock test.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days, open it every few days, and send me anything that's broken or confusing.
