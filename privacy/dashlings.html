Dashlings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Dashlings is a free game made by Vivek Kartha. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Dashlings does not ask for, collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (crystals, unlocked runners, settings, scores and similar game data) is saved only on your own phone, using the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. It can make your phone vibrate during play, which you can switch off in Settings.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a run, your phone's own share menu opens with a short message about your distance. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Children

Dashlings is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if advertising is added in a future version, this page will be updated before that version is released.
